What I do

Turn complex material into simple tools

Created a no-code tool for scientifically valid AI evals and red-teaming.

AI Safety Camp / AI evals tool

The tool can lead to discoveries of a wider variety of safety issues, by enabling domain experts with no coding/ML skills to run evals.

I did end-to-end user research, including personas, user tests and user interface design.

The tool isn't public yet, but is set to become so.

Test ideas fast and cheap - before they get expensive

By user testing a prototype, I found that the app the Danish Police had planned wouldn't solve their actual problem.

Danish National Police / Theft checker app

The client ended up saving months of work developing a solution that wouldn't work.

So the week we spent creating interactive prototypes, user testing and interviewing users ended up saving the client a lot of time and effort because it revealed a mismatch between the problem and the imagined solution.

Boil complicated subject matter down to plain language

Wrote the copy for the effective-giving platform Giv Effektivt. Based on the questions donors actually ask, not academic arguments.

Giv Effektivt / Website copy & design

The arguments for effective giving are usually explained quite academically. I cut the reading level and length down drastically to appeal to a broader audience (including people who are just busy).

I also built each page based on two questions: 'what do the readers want to know?' and 'what does the organization want them to do?'

The platform reached all its goals for member and donations within its first half year of existence.

I also cofounded and chaired Giv Effektivt.

Prioritize based on evidence, not opinions

As product owner on Denmark's official platform for companies I used research to guide our development efforts.

Danish Business Authority / Virksomhedsguiden

For instance, we had ~1,000 people vote on the topics they most needed help with before we created any content. This was paired with 40 interviews with different types of business owners - together the results decided what to focus on first.

By identifying the pages likely to draw the most traffic and making those pages really good, we managed to create a useful platform on a strict deadline - without getting any complaints from the public, media, or politicians, which isn't always the case for public project of this size and attention.

Design robust systems people can easily use, even under pressure

Designed the IT systems behind all national elections in Denmark. They've been used for two elections now, with no reported issues.

KOMBIT / Danish election system

My team built all the systems from scratch. We followed a structured process: design > workshop with users > iterate > user test > iterate > ship.

This level of early and continuous user involvement meant that we never shipped anything that wasn't understandable and useful to the users - even when they only use them less than once a year.

During the project I facilitated more than a hundred user tests and a dozen workshops.
